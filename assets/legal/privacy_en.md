**My SUES Privacy Policy**

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Your privacy is important to My SUES. This is a personal utility tool independently developed by a third-party developer. This policy explains how the app collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. Please read it carefully before using the app.

### 1. Core Principle: Local Data

We follow a local-data principle. **My SUES does not operate any independent backend server that stores your sensitive personal information.** All data obtained or produced through the app is stored only on your device.

### 2. Nature of the App

This app is an independent third-party personal utility tool and is **not an official product of, or authorized by, any university**. It has no affiliation, commission, or partnership with any university or any of its administrative departments.

### 3. How Accounts and Passwords Are Handled

- **Sign-in method:** The app accesses the **specific academic affairs system of a specific university** through an embedded web page (WebView). You complete authentication on that university's academic-system sign-in page.
- **Direct interaction:** All credentials you enter, such as your account ID and password, are entered and submitted by you directly on the sign-in page of the **specific university's academic affairs system website**. The app does not process, collect, or store any of your account credentials.
- **Nothing kept locally:** The app does not store, cache, or record anything related to your account ID, password, or other credentials on your device.
- **Never uploaded:** We promise that we will **never** upload your account ID, password, or other credentials to any server.

### 4. Academic Data We Process

- **Academic data:** The app only displays academic data (schedules, grades, exam arrangements, etc.) obtained from the **specific academic affairs system of a specific university**, so that you can view it offline. The app does not upload such data to any server.

### 5. Local Storage and Deletion

- **Local cache:** Retrieved data such as schedules and grades is stored in your device's local storage so that you can view it offline.
- **Data deletion:** You may remove locally stored academic data at any time through "Clear All Data." Uninstalling the app also removes all local cached data.

### 6. Permissions

- **Network:** used to access the **specific academic affairs system website of a specific university** (to retrieve data).
- **Storage (optional):** requested only when you use features such as "Export Schedule/Calendar" that need to save files.

### 7. Third-Party Services and Analytics

To continuously improve the app and fix crashes, the app may integrate anonymous third-party analytics tools (such as Sentry, Firebase Crashlytics, etc.). These tools collect only crash logs and feature-usage frequency data that do not contain personal identity information. This policy will be updated if additional third-party services are introduced.

### 8. Security Risks

- Because the app interacts directly with the **specific academic affairs system of a specific university**, although we adopt mainstream security technologies, we cannot control the security of network transmission on the university's academic-system servers (especially if the university system supports only HTTP).
- Do not use the app on rooted, jailbroken, or otherwise untrusted devices, as this may allow local data to be stolen by malware.

### 9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or the protection of your information, contact the developer through the feedback channel in the app. We will respond as soon as possible after receiving your inquiry.
