**My SUES Privacy Policy**

Last updated: February 8, 2026

Your privacy is important to My SUES. This is an unofficial utility independently developed by students of Shanghai University of Engineering Science. This policy explains how the app processes, stores, and protects your information.

### 1. Core Principle: Local Data

We follow a local-data principle. **My SUES does not operate an independent backend server that stores your sensitive personal information.**

### 2. Information We Process

- **Student ID and password:** used only to authenticate you with the university's academic system.
- **Academic data:** including schedules, grades, and exam arrangements, used only to provide app features.

### 3. Password Security

- Your password is loaded temporarily in device memory only when you actively sign in or refresh data and is used to send requests to the university system.
- We do not upload your account, password, schedule, exam, grade, or profile information to a developer-operated server.
- The app does not persistently store your academic-system password. Retrieved academic data is stored only on your device.

### 4. Local Storage and Deletion

- Schedule and grade data may be cached locally so that it remains available offline.
- You may remove local academic data through “Clear All Data.” Uninstalling the app also removes its local cache.

### 5. Permissions

- **Network:** connects to the university academic system.
- **Photos or storage (optional):** used only when selecting an avatar or background, or exporting a file.
- **Notifications (optional):** used only when you enable class or exam reminders.

### 6. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The app may use anonymous crash-reporting tools to improve reliability. Such tools process only crash logs and anonymous usage data that do not directly identify you. This policy will be updated if additional third-party services are introduced.

### 7. Security Risks

- My SUES communicates directly with university systems and cannot control every security risk in university servers or network connections.
- Do not process sensitive information on rooted, jailbroken, or otherwise untrusted devices.

### 8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the protection of your information, contact the developer through the feedback channel in the app.
