**My SUES Privacy Policy**

Last updated: September 9, 2026
Effective date: September 9, 2026

Your privacy is important to My SUES (hereinafter "the App"). The App is a personal utility tool developed and maintained by an independent third-party developer, Junhua Wang (hereinafter "we" or "the Developer"), and is **not an official product of, endorsed by, or authorized by any university**. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information. Please read it carefully, especially the parts in **bold**, before using the App. By tapping "Agree" or actually using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this policy in full.

### Core Principle: Local Data

We follow a local-data principle. **The App does not operate any independent backend server.** All data obtained or produced through the App is stored only on your device. Except when you actively visit your university's academic-affairs system website while using the App, **the App does not upload any of your data to any server or third party.**

### 1. How We Collect and Use Your Information

The App follows a **minimum-necessary** principle and does not collect any personal information in the background. The information described below arises from your own active use of a feature or is returned by your university's academic-affairs system, and is stored only on your device.

**1.1 Sign-in and data synchronization**

- **Sign-in method:** The App accesses the academic-affairs system website of **your university** through an embedded web page (WebView). You complete authentication on the university's academic-system sign-in page.
- **Account and password:** All credentials you enter, such as your account ID and password, are entered and submitted by you directly on the **university academic-system sign-in page** to the university. The App does **not** process, collect, store, or upload such credentials, nor does it store, cache, or record anything related to your account ID or password on your device.
- **Session cookies:** To keep you signed in to the university's academic-affairs system and to retrieve data, the session cookies returned by the system are saved on your device (managed by the system web component or the App's local session storage). They are used **only to maintain your sign-in session and retrieve data**, and for no other purpose. When you perform "Clear All Data" or uninstall the App, these session cookies are cleared as well.

**1.2 Academic data (schedules, grades, exams)**

To provide you with the ability to view and browse class schedules, grade transcripts (including GPA), and exam arrangements offline, the App saves such academic data on your device, together with related information such as your student ID, name, college, major, and class. **Such information is stored only on your device and is not uploaded by the App to any server.** For how your university's academic-affairs system processes its own data, please refer to your university's relevant rules.

**1.3 Information you edit voluntarily (optional)**

When you use features such as "My Profile," you may enter a nickname, college, major, or class, or pick an image from your photo library as an avatar or custom background. The profile information and image files you set are stored only on your device. Whether to provide, edit, or replace them is entirely up to you.

**1.4 Reminders and personalization**

When you set up course/exam reminders, theme, language, or other preferences, those settings and your custom background image are stored only on your device, and are used locally to show reminders or the interface according to your preferences. The App does not upload this content.

**1.5 App integrity self-check**

To warn you about the security of the running environment, the App only reads the App's own signing information on your device for a local check. It does **not** collect device identifiers or transmit anything over the network, and the result is used only to show a local security notice.

### 2. Permissions

The App requests permissions **only when you use the corresponding feature and after you grant consent**, and the system dialog explains the purpose at that time. The App never requests permissions before you agree or at startup without notice. In detail:

- **Network:** used to access your university's academic-affairs system website and to retrieve/synchronize class schedules, grades, and exam arrangements.
- **Notifications:** used to send you local notifications for the course/exam reminders you have set. Requested and used only when you enable the relevant reminder feature.
- **Photo / media library:** used only when you pick an image as your avatar or custom background.

### 3. Third-Party Services and Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, share, transfer, or publicly disclose your personal information to any third party, except: where required by law or a competent authority; or where necessary to access the **academic-affairs system of your university** that you actively designate. Apart from that, your personal information does not leave your device.

**The App currently integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, push-notification, or social SDKs that collect personal information.** The third-party open-source components the App relies on (such as basic UI, networking, and storage libraries) are used only to implement features locally and do not transmit your personal information to them; for the full list and licenses, see "Open-Source Licenses" inside the App. If, in the future, we introduce any third-party service that collects personal information, we will **update this policy first and obtain your consent again through an in-app dialog or equivalent**.

### 4. Storage and Security of Your Information

- **Where stored:** All data obtained or produced by the App is stored in your device's local storage (the app sandbox and local system preferences). No server-side storage is used.
- **Retention:** Your data is retained until you actively clear it or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App also removes the data the App stores locally on your device.
- **Protection:** Data is kept inside the operating system's app-isolation sandbox. We also recommend that you protect your device with the system's screen lock, biometrics, or similar measures.
- **Risk notice:** Because the App interacts directly with **your university's academic-affairs system**, although we adopt mainstream security technologies, we cannot control the security of network transmission on the university's server side (especially if the university system supports only HTTP). Do not use the App on rooted, jailbroken, or otherwise untrusted devices, as this may allow locally stored data to be stolen by malware.

### 5. Your Rights

- **View and correct:** You can view and correct the local profile information you entered in "Me" → Profile.
- **Delete data:** You may delete the class schedules, grades, profile and preference settings saved locally by the App at any time via "Clear All Data" under Settings, together with local files such as your avatar and custom background, and your sign-in session information. **Uninstalling the App also deletes all local data.**
- **Withdraw consent / refuse:** In the first-launch dialog, you may choose "Disagree and Exit" to stop using the App. You may also turn off independent features such as in-app reminders at any time, or revoke permissions (e.g., notifications, photo library) the App has obtained through your system settings. Withdrawing does not affect features that do not depend on the relevant permission.
- **About accounts:** The App maintains no separate account system. The management of your university academic-system account (including deregistration) is governed by your university's rules.
- **Responding to your requests:** If you have any questions or requests about this policy or your personal information, please contact us as described in Section 8. We will respond to your request within **15 working days** of receiving it.

### 6. Protection of Minors

The App is intended for current university students. If you are under the age of 18, please use the App only after a parent or guardian has read and agreed to this policy with you; if you are a child under the age of 14, please use the App only under the explicit consent and guidance of a parent or guardian. The App does not proactively collect personal information from minors. The information it processes comes from the academic-affairs system of the school you attend or is provided by you voluntarily.

### 7. Updates to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. In the event of a **material change** (such as a material change to the categories of information collected, the purposes of processing, or third-party sharing), we will inform you again and obtain your consent through an in-app dialog or equivalent. The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy governs the latest version. If you do not agree to the updated policy, please stop using the App.
